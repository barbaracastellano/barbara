English in a Connected World

English and digital technology influence the way people communicate, study, exchange information, and participate in modern society.

Explore the topics →
05

Main topics connecting English, technology, knowledge, commerce, and education.

QUESTION 01

How is English used around the world?

English has become an important tool for communication between people who may have different first languages.

A common language for connection

English can be found in many areas of international life. Its use is not limited to native speakers because people often use it as a shared language when communicating across countries.

01

International communication

People may use English when communicating with others whose native language is different.

02

Education and research

English is present in many academic resources, publications, and international courses.

03

Travel and culture

English can be useful when traveling and accessing cultural content from different countries.

QUESTION 02

What is the relationship between English and technology?

Digital technology and English have developed together, creating a strong connection between language and technological communication.

💻

Digital Tools

Software, websites, applications, and computer systems frequently contain English terms and instructions.

🔎

Online Information

English gives users access to a wide variety of websites, articles, tutorials, and digital resources.

👨‍💻

Programming

Many programming languages and technical concepts use words derived from English.

📡

Digital Communication

English is frequently used in online communities, social platforms, and international digital communication.

🧪

Science & Innovation

Scientists and technology professionals often use English to exchange research and technical information.

🌐

Global Access

Knowing English can make it easier to understand and navigate many international digital services.

QUESTION 03

How does technology help people share knowledge?

Digital tools have changed how information is created, stored, accessed, and exchanged.

🌎

Knowledge can travel beyond borders

A student in one country can access educational materials created in another country. Teachers, researchers, and communities can also collaborate without being in the same physical location.

📖

Digital Libraries

Online collections provide access to books, articles, documents, and other educational materials.

🤝

Collaboration

Shared documents and online platforms allow several people to work together on the same project.

💬

Online Communities

Communities allow people to exchange experiences, questions, explanations, and ideas.

🎓

Educational Resources

Students can find tutorials, courses, explanations, exercises, and other learning materials online.

QUESTION 04

How has technology changed commerce?

Digital technology has created new ways for businesses and consumers to interact.

Shopping beyond the traditional store

Commerce is no longer limited to physical locations. Websites and mobile platforms allow businesses to display products and communicate with customers through digital channels.

🛍️

Online Shopping

Customers can explore and purchase products through digital stores.

💳

Electronic Payments

Digital payment systems make online transactions possible.

📣

Digital Marketing

Businesses can present products through websites and social platforms.

🌍

International Markets

Digital platforms can connect businesses with customers in different countries.

QUESTION 05

How can people learn English online?

Online learning combines language practice with digital resources that students can access from different places and devices.

STEP 01

Watch & Listen

Videos, audio materials, interviews, and podcasts can expose learners to different forms of spoken English.

STEP 02

Read

Digital articles, stories, news, and educational texts can help develop reading comprehension and vocabulary.

STEP 03

Practice

Interactive exercises can help learners work with grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, and writing.

STEP 04

Communicate

Online communities and digital communication tools can provide opportunities to use English with others.
